We combine rigorous academic instruction with evidence-based strategies so every student develops the habits, organization, and mindset to thrive inside and outside the classroom.

Meet the Founder & Director

Rochelle Concepcion, M.A.

Special Education Specialist, RBT, & Founder of AGC Tutorials

With over a decade of experience across public school systems, specialized behavioral health, and one-on-one instruction, Rochelle established AGC Tutorials to transform how students learn, grow, and succeed. Her blend of educational expertise and analytical engineering training gives her a highly structured approach to diagnosing learning barriers and crafting data-driven intervention plans.

As a Special Education Case Manager, Classroom Teacher, and RBT, she has specialized in supporting students with autism, intellectual disabilities, ADHD, attentional challenges, and executive functioning deficits — with many students gaining multiple grade levels in reading and mathematics.
